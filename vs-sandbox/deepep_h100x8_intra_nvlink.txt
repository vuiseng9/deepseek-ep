Why 24 SMs?

    On H100 GPUs, there are 132 SMs total, but for communication kernels like this, 
    they're likely using a subset to avoid saturating all GPU resources while testing the NVLink bandwidth. 
    Using 24 SMs (roughly 18% of the GPU) leaves headroom for the actual communication to be the bottleneck rather than compute.
