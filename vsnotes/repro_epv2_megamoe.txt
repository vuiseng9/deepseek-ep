TFLOPS:	Raw measured throughput — 2 * num_recv_tokens * (hidden * intermediate_hidden * 3) / t_fused — covering the 3 matmuls (L1 gate+up projections, L2 down projection)
overlap: Compute-only throughput, approximating what GEMM achieves if the serial reduce-scatter overhead (t_reduction) is subtracted: tflops * t_fused / (t_fused - t_reduction)
HBM bandwidth utilization over t_fused, counting weights (FP4), activations (FP8), and outputs (BF16) read/written per expert touched — also scaled by the same approx_factor
NVLink bandwidth utilization over t_fused, counting dispatch (pull) + combine (write-back): num_recv_tokens * hidden * 3 bytes — also scaled by approx_factor
Wall-clock time of the fused MegaMoE kernel (t_fused)
Estimated serial time for the combine/reduce-scatter step: num_tokens * hidden * 2 * (1 + num_topk) / 6.5e12 — this is the overhead subtracted to get the "overlap" numbers
1.52x legacy	Speedup of the fused kernel vs. the baseline (tilelang + deep_ep separate dispatch/compute/combine)
